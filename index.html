AI Banking AgentOne assistant that never closes

Mobile, web, or branch kiosk. It understands what the customer wants and completes the next step, at any hour.

Today a simple question costs a customer three trips: a call center queue, a product brochure, a branch visit. Below, one customer opens the app at 02:14 — the call center is closed and the branch opens in seven hours.

What it does

  • Knows the customerReads a sample account balance and recent activity in a safe demo, so the customer feels this is my bank, talking to me.
  • Explains products in plain languageFees, benefits, eligibility, and a straight comparison of the options that actually apply — not a long PDF.
  • Books the branch visitReserves a slot with date, time, and purpose: onboarding, loan advice, or card pickup, plus a confirmation the customer keeps.

Staff stay in control

The agent drafts and guides; it does not decide. Every path that touches money, risk, or a customer record stops at a person.

  • Read onlyBalances come from a sample account in the demo build.
  • Draft, not sendAdvice and appointment details are prepared for staff to confirm.
  • Approval gateAnything that moves money waits for human sign-off.
  • LoggedEvery conversation is reviewable, so staff see what was said.

Why it matters for PPCBank

Not three separate tools bolted together — one Future Banking experience that changes what a branch visit is for.

  • The bank is open at 2 AM. Questions get answered when customers have them.
  • Product education that lands. Plain language, with the comparison needed to decide.
  • A shorter path to the appointment. The customer arrives informed, with a purpose recorded.
  • The branch becomes a conversation, not a queue. Staff spend time on high-value advice.

Demonstration data. All figures, accounts, and appointments shown are samples. No live customer account is read or changed in the demo build.
